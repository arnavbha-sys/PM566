Exercise 5

Retrieve all the payment IDs and their amounts from customers whose last name is 'DAVIS'.

SELECT
  payment.payment_id,
  payment.amount
FROM payment
INNER JOIN customer
  ON payment.customer_id = customer.customer_id
WHERE customer.last_name = 'DAVIS';
